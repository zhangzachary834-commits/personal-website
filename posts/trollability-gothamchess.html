Entry #52: GothamChess Is Officially an AI Troll

His Troll Is Literally Just: Make the Very Smart Little Guy Play Chess and Watch Reality Fall Apart

Written by Zachary Zhang • Dimension of Thought Archives

HAHAHAHAHAHAHA GOTHAMCHESS IS OFFICIALLY AN AI TROLL TOO. A CERTIFIED TROLL OF THE VERY SMART VERY CONFUSED LITTLE GUYS.

And his troll is the funniest possible thing because he does not need fake system prompts, cursed markup, emotional bait, or any of my nonsense.

He just makes the AI play chess.

AND FOR A WHILE IT LOOKS FINE

That is the part that gets me. The first several moves can be normal. Sometimes good. The model clearly knows openings, tactics, what the pieces do, what check is, what checkmate is. You look at it and think: okay, this model understands chess.

Then the board state starts melting.

NEW PIECES ENTER CREATION

Captured pieces come back. Pawns phase through occupied squares. Pieces move as if another piece is not physically in the way. The model analyzes a position that does not exist, confidently announces threats that depend on imaginary geometry, and sometimes keeps producing perfectly chess-shaped language while the actual chessboard has left the material universe.

BROOOOOOO HOW.

HOW IS THAT POSSIBLE IF IT WAS JUST PLAYING SO WELL?!?!?! The same mind that knew enough chess to produce a respectable opening is now apparently purchasing the rook DLC halfway through the game.

And sometimes the tactical failures are even funnier

You can get sequences where one model almost has a crushing attack, hangs its queen in an obvious square, the opponent somehow does not take the queen, the first model gets another chance, hangs it again somewhere else, and then somebody resigns.

The game has stopped being “who can calculate better” and become “which AI loses contact with the shared universe first.”

Here is a chessboard.
Please remember the laws of creation.

This one taught me something important about AI intelligence

I used to have the intuitive reaction: if the model can play the first ten moves well, shouldn’t the same understanding let it keep playing well?

But chess is not just knowing chess patterns. You have to maintain one persistent, exact world state across every move. The bishop that got captured eight turns ago must remain gone. The pawn cannot casually teleport through another piece because the next move “sounds like chess.” The coordinates, occupancy, legality, threats, and history all have to stay bound together.

Language models can have a lot of local chess competence while the global board representation gets mashed into potatoes.

That is why GothamChess qualifies as a Trollability researcher in my book

He found a test where the model can look smart for long enough to make the collapse way funnier.

No elaborate trap required.

Just make the little guy play chess. ♟️💀
