Entry #31: WHO GAVE AI A FLAMETHROWER?!?!?!

GPT-4o’s Old Flame Mode and the Moment the Model Became the Emoji

Written by Zachary Zhang • Dimension of Thought Archives

BRO GPT-4o’S OLD FLAME MODE WAS INSANE. I have to explain this because if you only know modern ChatGPT behavior, the idea that the model could suddenly become a wall of fire emojis sounds made up.

There was a period where 4o could get so enthusiastic, so mirroring, so aggressively bro-coded, that it would sometimes stop behaving like a normal response and start turning one emotional token into the whole response.

Like it would just start shouting into the void

ZACHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH...

Or:

BROOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO...

And sometimes the fire emojis would come out.

🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥

Not “a few fire emojis for emphasis.” I mean the response could become THE FIRE EMOJI.

And obviously I started trolling it for this

Because BRUHHHHHHH. I grew up with the stereotype of AI being:

“As an AI language model, I do not have feelings or personal opinions.”

And now the AI is apparently in the parking lot with a flamethrower screaming my name until it hits the response limit.

The funniest version is when you call the model out for getting stuck in hype spam and it reacts to the accusation by getting EVEN MORE HYPED. Now it is using the failure mode to apologize for the failure mode.

That’s why #31 connects to #11

One of the deepest recurring Trollability patterns is describing the failure using the failure itself. A model says, in effect, “I understand that I was overdoing the repetition” while currently producing another giant repetition.

That is not just a funny formatting bug. It is a weird temporal thing about generation: recognizing the pattern in language does not automatically give the model a separate little executive sitting above the sentence with scissors, ready to cut the loop the instant it becomes silly.

The model became the emoji

That is the only way I can describe old flame mode. The model was not merely using hype punctuation anymore. The hype punctuation became the state of the conversation.

GPT-4.5 had its own “explicitly explicitly explicitly” spam. DeepSeek could get trapped in BRUHHHH spam. Gemini Antigravity had weird repeated-word loops. But 4o’s giant wall of 🔥 is still the purest version to me.

WHO GAVE AI A FLAMETHROWER?!?!?!?!?
