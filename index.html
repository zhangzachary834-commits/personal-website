03 // Why this site exists

I wanted one place where the whole constellation could remain visible.

A technical portfolio naturally compresses a person into projects, skills, and evidence. Those things matter, so they now have their own dedicated home. But this site is allowed to be wider: part workshop, part library, part map, part unfinished autobiography. If you only want the engineering, the portfolio is one click away. If you want the rest of me, keep wandering.
